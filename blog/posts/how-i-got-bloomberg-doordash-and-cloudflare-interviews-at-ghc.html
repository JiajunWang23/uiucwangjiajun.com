𝐇𝐨𝐰 𝐈 𝐠𝐨𝐭 𝐁𝐥𝐨𝐨𝐦𝐛𝐞𝐫𝐠, 𝐃𝐨𝐨𝐫𝐃𝐚𝐬𝐡, 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐂𝐥𝐨𝐮𝐝𝐟𝐥𝐚𝐫𝐞 𝐢𝐧𝐭𝐞𝐫𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰𝐬 𝐚𝐭 𝐆𝐇𝐂

I attended the Grace Hopper Celebration in both 2023 and 2025.
In 2023, I had an onsite interview with Bank of America.
In 2025, I received interviews from Bloomberg, DoorDash, and Cloudflare.

With GHC 2026 coming up, here are six things I learned from attending GHC twice:

𝟏. 𝐔𝐩𝐥𝐨𝐚𝐝 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐮𝐦𝐞 𝐚𝐬 𝐞𝐚𝐫𝐥𝐲 𝐚𝐬 𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐛𝐥𝐞.
Companies actively search the resume database and may reach out before the conference even starts.
Replit, for example, has already started reaching out to candidates this year.
Do not wait until the conference begins.

𝟐. 𝐎𝐧 𝐃𝐚𝐲 𝟏, 𝐠𝐨 𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐚𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭 𝐭𝐨 𝐁𝐥𝐨𝐨𝐦𝐛𝐞𝐫𝐠.
If your goal is to get interviews directly from GHC, Bloomberg should be one of your first stops.

𝟑. 𝐀𝐟𝐭𝐞𝐫 𝐁𝐥𝐨𝐨𝐦𝐛𝐞𝐫𝐠, 𝐭𝐚𝐫𝐠𝐞𝐭 𝐨𝐭𝐡𝐞𝐫 𝐜𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐚𝐧𝐢𝐞𝐬 𝐭𝐡𝐚𝐭 𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞𝐥𝐲 𝐫𝐞𝐜𝐫𝐮𝐢𝐭 𝐚𝐭 𝐆𝐇𝐂.
Some companies I would prioritize include:
• Bank of America
• USAA
• Cloudflare
• DoorDash
• Intuit
Don’t spend 40 minutes waiting at a booth just because the company has a famous name.
Your goal is not to collect swag.
Your goal is to create interview opportunities.

𝟒. 𝐏𝐫𝐞𝐩𝐚𝐫𝐞 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐩𝐢𝐭𝐜𝐡 𝐟𝐨𝐫 𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐲 𝐜𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐚𝐧𝐲.
Before entering the expo hall, you should already be ready to answer:
• Why this company?
• Why this position?
• Why should we hire you?
• What can you contribute to the team?

Saying “I’m looking for a software engineering role” is easy to forget.
Showing that you understand what the company is building makes a much stronger impression.
𝟓. 𝐈𝐟 𝐲𝐨𝐮 𝐝𝐨𝐧’𝐭 𝐠𝐞𝐭 𝐚𝐧 𝐢𝐧𝐭𝐞𝐫𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐟𝐢𝐫𝐬𝐭 𝐭𝐢𝐦𝐞, 𝐭𝐚𝐥𝐤 𝐭𝐨 𝐬𝐨𝐦𝐞𝐨𝐧𝐞 𝐞𝐥𝐬𝐞.
This is probably the biggest lesson I learned.
Cloudflare is the best example.
On the last day of GHC, I talked to around five different people at the Cloudflare booth before I finally received an interview opportunity.
If I had stopped after the first conversation, I would never have gotten that interview.
But different recruiters and engineers may represent different teams, locations, or hiring needs.
Persistence matters.

𝟔. 𝐅𝐨𝐥𝐥𝐨𝐰 𝐮𝐩 𝐰𝐢𝐭𝐡 𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐲𝐨𝐧𝐞 𝐲𝐨𝐮 𝐦𝐞𝐞𝐭.
Add them on LinkedIn.
Send a short message reminding them:
• Where you met
• What you talked about
• Which role you are interested in
GHC only lasts a few days.
The connections you build there can last much longer.

The biggest lesson I learned from attending GHC twice:
𝐃𝐨𝐧’𝐭 𝐣𝐮𝐬𝐭 𝐚𝐭𝐭𝐞𝐧𝐝 𝐆𝐇𝐂.

That approach helped me get interview opportunities with Bank of America, Bloomberg, DoorDash, and Cloudflare.
Hope this helps anyone preparing for GHC 2026. 🚀
