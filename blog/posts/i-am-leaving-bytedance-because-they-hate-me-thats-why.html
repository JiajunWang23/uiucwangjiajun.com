Because my three-month internship is ending.
That’s the actual reason. Nobody hates me. 😂

Real users. Real responsibility. And people willing to help me become a better engineer.

To everyone who answered my questions, challenged my assumptions, and supported my work: thank you.

My biggest takeaway? Take your work seriously. Take your health seriously, too. Neither requires sacrificing the other.
The internship ends. The learning doesn’t. ❤️
